    Side A: The Contractor <-> Agent (WhatsApp Control Plane)

         The Interaction: The contractor never logs into a web app. When they finish an estimate on Sunday night, they forward the PDF, text a photo
         of a handwritten work order, or send a 10-second voice note into a dedicated WhatsApp business number.

         The UX: The agent acts as an executive assistant sitting on their phone. It confirms data extraction, asks for one-tap approvals via WhatsApp
         interactive buttons or quick replies, and pings them only when a high-ticket customer responds with buying intent.

    Side B: The Agent <-> Customer (Carrier-Compliant Plain-Text Channel)

         The Delivery: The customer should generally receive plain-text SMS (via Twilio/Telnyx) rather than an unsolicited WhatsApp message.

         The Reason: Meta enforces strict 24-hour customer-care windows on WhatsApp. Reaching out cold 5 days after an estimate requires pre-
         approved template marketing categories that look corporate and trigger spam fatigue. A plain-text SMS from a local 10-digit number looks like
         an owner texting from their truck cab.

         Safety Guardrail: The customer is never allowed to chat autonomously with an unconstrained LLM about scope, pricing, or engineering
         tolerances. If the customer asks a question, the agent alerts the contractor on WhatsApp to step in.

    4. Critical Traps to Avoid During Implementation

    While the concept is commercially sound, three common design flaws can derail the project:

       1. Do Not Let the LLM Write Unbounded Outbound Quotes:

         Never let an agent dynamically calculate or alter quotes. If a shed quote is $25,000, keep the dollar amount and line items as immutable literal
         variables. If an LLM hallucinates and texts a customer an altered price or promises an unverified delivery schedule, the contractor will face
         severe legal and financial liability.

       2. Beware the WhatsApp 24-Hour Session Limit:

         You cannot build a multi-week follow-up cadence that initiates unprompted WhatsApp chats with customers without using Meta-approved pre-
         paid message templates. Keep WhatsApp as the management interface for the contractor, and use local plain-text SMS (via Twilio/Telnyx)
         for customer-facing outreach.

       3. Keep Deterministic Workflows Out of the Agent Loop:

         Use LLMs for what they do best: unstructured PDF extraction, tone formatting, and intent categorization. Do not use an autonomous LLM loop
         to decide when to wait 3 days. Use deterministic state tracking (PostgreSQL or Redis queues with LangGraph checkpointers) to govern the
         cron intervals and wait states.

    The Commercial Reality
    Positioning this as an "AI Agent App" will encounter the standard blue-collar resistance to software overhead.

    Positioning it as "The $399/month Virtual Estimating Assistant that lives inside your WhatsApp"—where all they have to do is forward their
    Sunday-night PDFs and reply "YES" to follow-up drafts—directly eliminates their operational fatigue while respecting their workflow.
